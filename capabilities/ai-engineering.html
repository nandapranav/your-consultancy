14 / FINAL CHECK

Before you ship.

A production AI system should answer more than “does the demo work?”

Can we explain what the system is allowed to do?

Permissions, tools, actions, and approval boundaries should be explicit rather than implied.

Can we tell when quality gets worse?

Evaluation and monitoring should make regressions visible before users discover them at scale.

Can we understand what a failure means?
